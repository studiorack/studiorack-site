FDNCymbal v0.2.22

By Takamitsu Endo

FDNCymbal is a cymbal sound synthesizer that can also be used as an effect. Most of the metallic texture comes from a Schroeder allpass section; the FDN (feedback delay network) section makes a nice impact sound. Tremolo simulates cymbal wobble.
