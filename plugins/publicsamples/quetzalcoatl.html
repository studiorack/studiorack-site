Quetzalcoatl v0.8.7

By Modular Samples

Quetzalcoatl is an 8-layer sample playback plugin (rompler) for Mac, Windows & Linux. Features multi-stage crossfading, wavetable mode, wavefolder, extensive modulation/sequencing, filter, and a large built-in sound library.
