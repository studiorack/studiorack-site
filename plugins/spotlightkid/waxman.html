Waxman v0.1.0

By Christopher Arndt

Waxman is a virtual guitar pre-amp and tone-stack comprising of a noise gate, compressor, distortion and equalizer stage. It is a multi-format, cross-platform audio effect plugin, emulating the sound of a Tom Scholz Rockman headphone practice amp.
