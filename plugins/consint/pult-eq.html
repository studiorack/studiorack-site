Pult EQ v1.0.0

By Consistent Interruption

Pult EQ is a free and open source Pultec style tube EQ VST3 plugin. Features low/high boost & attenuation (Pultec trick), mid/side & stereo modes, tube saturation with auto gain and up to 16x oversampling. For adding mojo and coloration.
