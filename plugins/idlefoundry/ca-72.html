CA-72 v0.2.0

By Idle Foundry

A circuit-modelled synthesizer based on the 1972–73 Minimoog Model D, with monophonic and polyphonic modes, stereo voices, MIDI Learn and three quality settings. VST3 and CLAP on macOS, Windows and Linux, plus Audio Unit on macOS.
