PerceptoMap v0.18.2

By Qirui Huang

PerceptoMap is a real-time psychoacoustic visualization VST3 plugin. Displays Mel spectrograms, MFCCs, Chromagram, Tempogram, Spectral Centroid, Contrast, Flatness, Rolloff, Onset markers, and more perceptual audio features.
