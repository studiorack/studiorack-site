Nyasynth v1.0.0

By Aaron Kofsky

Nyasynth is a recreation / modern port of the famous Meowsynth (cat meow synthesizer). A fun, meme-inspired VST3 instrument with cat vocal synthesis. Available for Windows and macOS (ARM + Universal).
