Zones v1.0.5

By Zones Convolution

Zones convolution allows users to find and share impulse responses captured in great sounding and unique spaces. Zones provides; a simple, intuitive interface to tag, categorise and upload IRs, and a VST/AU plugin to search and convolve these IRs directly
