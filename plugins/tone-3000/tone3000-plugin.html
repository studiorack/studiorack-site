TONE3000 Plugin v0.0.2

By TONE3000

A JUCE-based audio plugin that loads Neural Amp Modeler (NAM) captures and impulse responses (IRs) from the TONE3000 online tone catalog, letting you browse, sign in, and add tones directly into your signal chain.
