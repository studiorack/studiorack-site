MIDI BPM Detection v0.1.0

By Vincent Alsteen

Estimates tempo (BPM) from incoming MIDI note-on events in realtime while you play, showing a histogram of competing tempo candidates. Includes a Bitwig controller extension to feed detected tempo back to the DAW transport. Experimental project.
