MeasureCounter v1.0.0

By geissmannsbin

VST3/AU plugin for live performance measure counting with beat-segmented progress bar. Shows current bar/beat, cycle progress (4/8/16/32 bars), and visual warnings. Perfect for staying in sync during shows and rehearsals.
