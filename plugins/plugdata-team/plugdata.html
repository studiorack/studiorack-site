plugdata v0.9.3-2

By plugdata-team

A plugin wrapper around Pure Data with a new GUI, allowing visual patching in a wide selection of DAWs. Ships with the ELSE collection of externals and abstractions, and can also be used as a standalone replacement for Pure Data.
